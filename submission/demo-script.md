# GhostTab — silent iPhone demo plan
DRAFT. No video has been recorded or uploaded.
Target: 60–90 seconds. Record the real hosted app in Safari, portrait, microphone OFF. No face, narration, music or slides required. Enable Do Not Disturb and avoid notifications or personal links. Use demo data only.
1. Open the hosted GhostTab URL and start iPhone screen recording. Hold on the title for 3 seconds.
2. Tap Load demo. Pause on the queue and current-link card.
3. Tap Mark read once. Pause so the current URL and progress change are visible.
4. Tap Skip once. Show the updated status.
5. Reload Safari. Show that progress and queue return unchanged.
6. Tap Copy pending links. Show the actual exported text (clipboard permission may change the toast; the text is still selectable).
7. Optionally mark the remaining demo links read, then hold on Queue Complete.
8. Stop recording and send the original video to ChatGPT for captions and trimming.
Do not show third-party destination pages in the video. Opening links is verified separately; destination logos are unnecessary.
